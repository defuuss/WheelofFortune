🎡 Wheel of Fortune

A cinematic weighted wheel for roleplay: visual spins, secret modes, character-triggered controls, Lorebook sourcing, cooldowns, and adaptive intensity levels.

🎨 Wheel appearance

🎭 Visibility / secrecy

Secret-result and Blind mode never reveal the selected forfeit through the UI, history list, toast, or a visible system message.

⏳ Suspense

The default spin is intentionally longer and makes 11–16 full turns before landing.
